When you need a tow truck

  • A breakdown that stops the car from going any further
  • After an accident — when the car can't move or isn't safe to drive
  • Seized engine, gearbox or clutch
  • Flat tyres with no spare, or a missing wheel
  • Moving a car to a garage, home or a new owner without driving it

How it works

  1. Call 0877 360 333 and tell us where you are, what car it is and what happened.
  2. We give you the price and an estimated arrival time.
  3. We load the car onto the flatbed and secure it for the trip.
  4. We take it wherever you need — a garage, your home, a car park.

Driving a rental car?

Most rental companies have their own assistance line — check your rental papers first. If they can't help quickly, or you'd rather arrange it yourself, call us directly.

What we carry

Frequently asked questions

How much is a tow truck in Sofia?

Within Sofia city — 60 € (117.35 BGN) for cars and SUVs up to 2000 kg. Outside the city — 1 € per km per direction. Between 21:00 and 07:00 and on public holidays a +50% night rate applies, e.g. 90 € instead of 60 €.

Is loading charged separately?

No. The price includes loading, transport and unloading.

How fast will the tow truck arrive?

It depends on where you are and on traffic. We give you an estimated arrival time when you call.
