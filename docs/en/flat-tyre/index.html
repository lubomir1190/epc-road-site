When not to change it yourself

  • On the motorway or a narrow hard shoulder, with traffic right next to you
  • When the jack or wheel brace is missing, or the bolts are stuck
  • In the dark, in the rain or on ice

No spare wheel?

Many newer cars come with only a repair kit, or nothing at all. If the tyre can't be fixed on the spot, we load the car onto the tow truck and take it to a tyre shop or any address you choose.

If the car is missing a wheel, loading costs an extra 50 € (97.79 BGN) per wheel.

On the motorway

While you wait, stand behind the crash barrier wearing a hi-vis vest — not next to the car. More tips on our motorway breakdown page.
