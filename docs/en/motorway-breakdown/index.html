What to do straight away

  1. Pull over as far right as you can — onto the hard shoulder or the verge.
  2. Switch on the hazard lights and put on your hi-vis vest before getting out.
  3. Place the warning triangle behind the car.
  4. Get out on the right-hand side and wait behind the crash barrier with your passengers.
  5. If anyone is hurt, call 112 first — the EU emergency number works in Bulgaria and operators speak English.
  6. Then call us on 0877 360 333 and tell us your direction of travel and the nearest landmark.

How to tell us where you are

On the motorway your “address” is the direction and the kilometre. Look for the kilometre markers by the road, or remember the last exit or petrol station you passed. You can also share your location from your phone.

Frequently asked questions

How much is towing from the motorway?

Outside Sofia city it's 1 € per km per direction. We confirm the exact price on the phone before we set off.

Should I stay in the car while I wait?

Not on the motorway. The safest place is behind the crash barrier, away from moving traffic.

Do you work on the motorway at night?

Yes, 24/7, including weekends and public holidays.
