Signs of a flat battery

  • The starter clicks but the engine doesn't turn over
  • Dashboard and lights are dim or dead
  • Central locking doesn't respond
  • The car has been parked for a long time, especially in the cold

While you wait

  • Switch off the headlights, air conditioning, radio and anything else that draws power.
  • Don't keep trying to start the engine — it drains the battery further.
  • On a busy street, switch on the hazard lights and put out the warning triangle.

If it still won't start

Sometimes the problem isn't the battery but the starter, the alternator or the electrics. Then we can load the car onto the tow truck and take it to a garage — no need to wait for a second vehicle.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a jump start?

What if the battery is completely dead?

A jump start works in most cases. If the battery won't hold a charge or the fault is elsewhere, we can tow the car to a garage.

Do you come out at night?

Yes, 24/7, including weekends and public holidays. A +50% night rate applies between 21:00 and 07:00.
