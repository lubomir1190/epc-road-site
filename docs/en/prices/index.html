What affects the price

  • Distance — outside Sofia city it's charged per kilometre
  • Time — night rate between 21:00 and 07:00 and on public holidays

  • Its condition — for example a missing wheel

Prices are in euro; BGN amounts are shown for reference. We confirm the final price on the phone before we set off.
