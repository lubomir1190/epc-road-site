When it makes sense

  • Taking a car to a garage for repairs or diagnostics
  • A car you've just bought — from the seller to you or to a garage
  • A car that has been parked for a long time and isn't roadworthy
  • A sports, classic or lowered car you'd rather not drive in traffic
  • Moving a car between two addresses

How booking works

  1. Pick a day and time in our online calendar.
  2. Enter the pick-up and drop-off addresses and the make and model of the car.
  3. We arrive at the agreed time.

Conditions

  • Cars and SUVs up to 2000 kg
  • Book at least 24 hours ahead
  • The discount is for planned transport only. In an emergency, call 0877 360 333.
